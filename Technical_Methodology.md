# Technical Methodology: The Engineering Approach

## The "Four-Phase" Framework
I approach every technical task—whether a home project or enterprise deployment—using the following iterative loop:

1.  **Discovery & Risk Assessment:** Identify the problem, assess potential business/security impact, and define success metrics.
2.  **Architecture & Design:** Develop a robust, scalable, and secure solution (utilizing frameworks like NIST CSF or CIS where applicable).
3.  **Implementation & Hardening:** Execute the build using industry-standard tools (Docker, VLAN segmentation, Least Privilege access) with a "Security-by-Design" focus.
4.  **Verification & Documentation:** Validate performance against the original problem statement and document the architecture for long-term sustainability.

## Engineering Mindset
*   **Forensic Readiness:** Design systems such that if a failure *does* occur, data is preserved and the incident is auditable.
*   **Resource Efficiency:** Optimize hardware utilization (e.g., containerization, compression, parity storage) to ensure sustainability and low operational overhead.
