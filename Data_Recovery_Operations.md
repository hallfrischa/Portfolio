# Incident Recovery & Business Continuity Operations

## Executive Summary
Executed high-stakes incident recovery operations for small businesses and individuals facing catastrophic storage failures, utilizing bare-metal extraction techniques to restore critical data and minimize operational downtime.

## Problem Statement
Hardware degradation (e.g., mechanical drive failure, impact damage) and logical corruption posed a severe threat of permanent data loss. The objective was to triage failing NTFS partitions and SD cards to recover sensitive assets before degradation became irreversible.

## Technical Execution & Privacy Controls
*   **Diagnostic Triage:** Rapidly assessed failing hardware components (Windows HDDs, SD cards) to determine viability for software-based extraction versus physical clean-room requirements.
*   **Secure Extraction Workflow:** Utilized File Scavenger to perform raw data extraction by slaving the degraded drive and a sterile target drive to a dedicated recovery terminal, circumventing the host OS.
*   **Data Confidentiality:** Maintained strict privacy controls by executing all recoveries entirely on-site. Data was transferred directly device-to-device with zero intermediate network storage, eliminating unauthorized exposure risks.
*   **Media Sanitization:** Guided clients on secure asset disposal, executing secure data wipes aligned with standard media sanitization practices when requested prior to hardware handoff.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Incident Response:** Developed the composure to manage high-pressure data loss scenarios and prioritize the preservation of sensitive files.
*   **Risk & Boundary Awareness:** Demonstrated a clear understanding of the boundary between operational recovery and formal forensic investigations, ensuring data integrity remained the primary focus.
*   **Data Privacy:** Prioritized strict on-site data handling workflows to protect personal and business-critical information from third-party exposure.
Hardware failure (e.g., hard drive mechanical failure, SD card corruption) posed a threat of permanent data loss for clients. The goal was to recover assets before data degradation became irreversible.

## Methodology
*   **Diagnostic Assessment:** Rapidly identified failing hardware components to prevent further data loss.
*   **Forensic Recovery:** Utilized recovery software (e.g., File Scavenger) to perform raw data extraction, unscramble corrupted file systems, and image drives.
*   **Integrity Verification:** Focused on the preservation of sensitive documents and critical system files, ensuring reliable restoration for the end user.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Incident Response:** Developed the composure and forensic intuition necessary to manage high-pressure data loss scenarios.
*   **Resiliency:** Proven ability to restore operational stability for clients when traditional methods fail.
*   **Data Integrity:** Focused on the core mission of data preservation and evidence-based recovery.
