# Project: Data Continuity & Forensic Recovery Operations

## Executive Summary
Executed high-stakes data recovery operations for clients facing catastrophic storage failures, utilizing forensic imaging and recovery tools to restore essential business data and minimize downtime.

## Problem Statement
Hardware failure (e.g., hard drive mechanical failure, SD card corruption) posed a threat of permanent data loss for clients. The goal was to recover assets before data degradation became irreversible.

## Methodology
*   **Diagnostic Assessment:** Rapidly identified failing hardware components to prevent further data loss.
*   **Forensic Recovery:** Utilized recovery software (e.g., File Scavenger) to perform raw data extraction, unscramble corrupted file systems, and image drives.
*   **Integrity Verification:** Focused on the preservation of sensitive documents and critical system files, ensuring reliable restoration for the end user.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Incident Response:** Developed the composure and forensic intuition necessary to manage high-pressure data loss scenarios.
*   **Resiliency:** Proven ability to restore operational stability for clients when traditional methods fail.
*   **Data Integrity:** Focused on the core mission of data preservation and evidence-based recovery.
