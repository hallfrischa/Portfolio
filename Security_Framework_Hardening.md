# Security Framework Alignment & Audit Preparation

## Executive Summary
Executed comprehensive governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) initiatives encompassing both academic capstone projects and practical defense-sector internship experience. This project highlights the ability to perform gap analyses, generate remediation plans, and author technical policies that strictly align with NIST SP 800-171 and NIST SP 800-53 control frameworks.

## Policy Development & NIST Alignment
*   **Net-New Policy Creation:** Authored a comprehensive Security Camera Policy from scratch to govern the physical deployment, data retention, and acceptable use of surveillance assets in compliance with organizational security standards.
*   **Legacy Policy Modernization:** Conducted targeted reviews of existing corporate security policies, mapping current operational controls against the rigorous requirements of NIST SP 800-171 and NIST SP 800-53. 
*   **Audit Readiness:** Updated and refined governance documentation to ensure the organization was properly postured for an upcoming formal compliance audit.

## Gap Analysis & Remediation (POA&M)
*   **Control Assessment:** Performed formal gap analyses across critical security domains to identify compliance shortfalls, with a heavy analytical focus on Access Control (AC), Physical and Environmental Protection (PE), and Incident Response (IR).
*   **Remediation Tracking:** Developed and structured Plans of Action and Milestones (POA&Ms) to systematically document vulnerabilities, assign resources, and track the remediation lifecycle of identified framework deficiencies.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Translating Controls to Operations:** Demonstrated the ability to interpret complex federal frameworks (NIST) and translate them into actionable, easily understood corporate policies.
*   **Full-Lifecycle GRC:** Showcased a holistic understanding of the compliance lifecycle—from initially auditing physical security and access controls to drafting the overarching policies that govern them.
