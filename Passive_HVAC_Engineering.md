# Project: Environmental Control & Passive HVAC Engineering

## Executive Summary
Engineered a specialized passive HVAC system utilizing custom PVC ventilation stacks to manage airflow and mitigate environmental degradation during the winterization of marine and recreational vehicles.

## Problem Statement
Sealed environments in RVs and boats are highly susceptible to moisture accumulation, mold, and structural degradation during long-term winter storage. Active electrical HVAC systems are often impractical or unsafe for extended, unmonitored off-grid storage.

## Technical Design
*   **Passive Airflow Dynamics:** Designed a ventilation architecture that leverages natural thermal convection and ambient air currents rather than powered mechanical blowers.
*   **Material Engineering:** Fabricated custom ventilation stacks using modified PVC components, chosen for their durability, thermal resistance, and cost-effectiveness.
*   **Moisture Mitigation:** Strategically routed airflow to prevent interior condensation and manage humidity levels across distinct climate zones inside the vehicle/vessel.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Multi-Disciplinary Engineering:** Showcased the ability to apply principles of fluid dynamics, environmental control, and mechanical fabrication to solve a complex physical problem.
*   **Resilient Design:** Developed a "fail-safe" passive solution that requires zero power consumption and zero active maintenance during deployment.
