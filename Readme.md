# Aaron Hallfrisch | Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Portfolio

## Executive Summary
I am a cybersecurity professional and systems engineer with over 15 years of hands-on IT experience and a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance. My expertise lies at the intersection of physical infrastructure, network defense, and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC).
I approach technical debt and security vulnerabilities using the "Leave It Better" principle—architecting environments that are secure-by-design, highly resilient, and meticulously documented.
