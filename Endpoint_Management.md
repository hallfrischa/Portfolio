# Project: Enterprise Endpoint Security & Lifecycle Management

## Executive Summary
Led a proactive remediation effort to standardize the enterprise endpoint fleet, ensuring all assets adhered to modern security baselines, firmware requirements, and OS lifecycle policies.

## Problem Statement
The organization faced a critical security risk due to a significant portion of the endpoint fleet running End-of-Life (EOL) operating systems and outdated firmware, increasing the attack surface and vulnerability to exploits.

## Implementation & Strategy
*   **Vulnerability Mitigation:** Spearheaded an initiative to transition endpoints from EOL software to supported, secure operating systems, including provisioning Windows 11 Pro environments on Microsoft Surface Book 3 Core i7 hardware.
*   **Infrastructure Coordination:** Acted as a process advocate to facilitate the deployment of updated, compliant imaging protocols via PXE.
*   **Data Synchronization & Security:** Configured Nextcloud and GitHub-synced Obsidian vaults across endpoint environments to ensure secure, encrypted, and decentralized access to critical documentation.
*   **Lifecycle Maintenance:** Executed standardized firmware (BIOS) updates and OS patching schedules to maintain system health and security integrity.
*   **Identity & Asset Management:** Managed Active Directory account lifecycles and location tracking to ensure operational efficiency and accurate resource allocation.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Security Discipline:** Demonstrated the ability to proactively identify and close "compliance gaps" within an enterprise environment.
*   **Process Advocacy:** Proven ability to navigate internal organizational silos to achieve critical security outcomes.
*   **Asset Hygiene:** Established a repeatable, high-standard workflow for endpoint imaging and maintenance.
