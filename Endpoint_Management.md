# Enterprise & SMB Endpoint Lifecycle Management

## Executive Summary
Leveraged extensive independent IT consulting experience alongside structured enterprise internships to secure and maintain endpoint fleets across highly varied environments. This dual experience bridged agile, compliance-driven SMB deployments (healthcare) with large-scale, tightly regulated corporate Active Directory infrastructure.

## Enterprise Infrastructure & Vulnerability Management
*   **Lifecycle Provisioning:** Streamlined bare-metal device provisioning and standardized imaging via PXE within a dedicated corporate Active Directory environment.
*   **Vulnerability Mitigation:** Executed remediation campaigns for End-of-Life (EOL) operating systems, systematically identifying and migrating vulnerable endpoints to supported security baselines.
*   **Patch Management:** Deployed enterprise-wide OS patches and firmware updates utilizing Microsoft SCCM and targeted scripting to maintain continuous compliance and system integrity.

## Healthcare & SMB Endpoint Security
*   **EDR Deployment:** Engineered and administered next-generation endpoint detection and response (EDR) platforms—specifically SentinelOne and Microsoft Defender for Business—across distributed client networks.
*   **Compliance-Driven IT:** Managed hardware lifecycles and endpoint security for private medical practices, ensuring infrastructure implementations aligned seamlessly with strict data privacy and HIPAA access requirements.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Operational Adaptability:** Demonstrated the ability to scale security controls from bespoke medical office domains to rigid, large-scale corporate networks.
*   **Asset Hygiene:** Established repeatable, high-standard workflows for endpoint imaging, proactive patching, and hardware lifecycle maintenance.
*   **Threat Surface Reduction:** Consistently applied modern EDR tooling and vulnerability management practices to minimize the physical and logical attack surface.
