# Network Infrastructure Audit & Hardware Validation

## Executive Summary
Conducted a comprehensive operational readiness assessment and security baseline on enterprise-grade Juniper networking hardware, ensuring the gear was vetted, cleared of legacy configurations, and prepared for secure integration into a production-ready lab environment.

## Problem Statement
The objective was to evaluate the integrity and functional status of legacy ("hand-me-down") network equipment. Without a proper audit and reset, this hardware posed both an operational risk (unknown configurations) and a security risk (potentially outdated firmware/vulnerabilities).

## Methodology
*   **Hardware Audit:** Performed physical inspection and verified power-on self-test (POST) integrity.
*   **Security Baseline:** Utilized console-based out-of-band management to perform a full factory reset, ensuring the removal of all legacy data and unauthorized configurations.
*   **Vulnerability Assessment:** Verified current firmware versions and assessed against known vulnerabilities to ensure the hardware met modern security standards.

## Technical Architecture (Sanitized)
*   **Access Method:** Out-of-band management via console cable and serial terminal emulation.
*   **Integrity Check:** Cleared configuration states and verified OS stability.
*   **Future Integration:** Prepared the hardware for deployment within a segmented VLAN network topology.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Hardware Lifecycle:** Demonstrated the ability to evaluate, vet, and standardize enterprise networking equipment.
*   **Security First:** Prioritized a secure "clean-slate" approach to hardware integration, mitigating risks associated with legacy or pre-owned networking devices.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Deepened experience with enterprise-grade networking command-line interfaces (CLI).
