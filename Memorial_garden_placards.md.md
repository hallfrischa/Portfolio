# Memorial Placard Inscriptions

**Sourcing & Aesthetic Recommendations:**
To match the rugged, north-woods character of the camp, consider Cast Bronze with a Dark Oxide Background[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). The raised letters remain metallic gold/bronze, while the recessed background is darkened[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Mount the entrance placards on heavy cedar posts set just to the right of each opening[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). Mount the center storyboard placard on a low, angled stone pedestal or a heavy cedar easel[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

## Part 1: The Entrance Placards
These are smaller, highly focused plaques positioned at the shoulders of the North-West and North-East entryways[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

## Part 2: The Center Storyboard Placard
This larger, prominent plaque sits in the clearing (near the benches or stone altar)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). It explains the architecture of the garden and how the physical design mirrors the story of your family[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

### The Convergence Glade

Welcome to this quiet loop of earth, sculpted from the wild to honor the hands and the hard work that held our family together[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).

Here, two paths begin apart, winding through the protective embrace of the Balsam Firs[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span). One path carries the quiet strength of a father who built our shelter; the other bears the nurturing grace of a mother who cultivated our lives[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). At the threshold, the trees bend, slowing our pace and turning our steps inward, until both paths converge into the sunlight together[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

At the heart of the glade stands our centerpiece tree, rooted upon a tiered stone altar[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span). In the autumn, we gather here to remember under a canopy of color; in the winter, its bare branches hold the snow, a symbol of life holding fast against the cold[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).

Framed against the forest rest the iron tools of those who worked this ground before us—timeless monuments to honest labor[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span). Just to the north stands the covered bridge, raised by father and son, bridging our past and our future[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).

May this circle forever be a sanctuary of peace, a testimony to a lifetime of partnership, and a living promise that those we love are never gone, but are the very soil in which we grow[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
